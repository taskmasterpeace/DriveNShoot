# WEB_DEATHS — the death library, and infected that ask three answers

**Status:** BUILT 2026-10-29 on `feat/web-deaths` (lane DEATHS + ZOMBIE-FUN, port 3488).
**Code:** rows in `web/src/data/deaths.js` (clips, ragdoll, vehicle tops, hood launch, headshot, sever, blood) and
`web/src/data/hordes.js` (breed answers, warnings, migration); baked clips in `web/src/data/deathclips-ual.js` (generated by
`web/scratchpad/deaths/bake-ual.mjs`). Systems: `web/src/systems/deaths.js` (clips, ragdoll, limbs, blood),
`web/src/systems/migrations.js` (highway hordes, dust, witnesses, the radio), the breed answers and warnings in
`web/src/systems/zombies.js`. The pose is drawn by `characters.js ragPose`. Agent surface: `web/src/agent/deaths-api.js`.
**Builds on:** `WEB_ZOMBIES.md` (breeds, senses, crawlers, drift), `WEB_DEATH_RITES.md` (decay and burial),
`WEB_BALLISTICS.md` (hit parts), `WEB_LSW_BODIES.md` (the near bodies), `WEB_DEPTH_LAYERS.md` (decals), `WEB_PULLS.md`
(the knowledge law and the radio).

## 1. Overview
Robert, 2026-10-29: "deaths sell the game", and "fun comes from switching how you play, not from more health". This lane
does five things.
- **The death library.** Every death plays a clip picked by how it happened:
  - shot from the front or the back;
  - a headshot;
  - a leg shot (it drops, crawls and dies);
  - blown up;
  - run over;
  - burned;
  - beaten (blunt) or cut down (blade).

  On its last frames each clip blends into a cheap verlet ragdoll. The ragdoll settles on what is really under the body
  (land, a stair, a truck bed, a car's hood), and death rites rots it to bones.
- **Guns that feel good on them.**
  - Limbs come off.
  - A leg gone makes a crawler, and it keeps coming.
  - A headshot drops one clean.
  - A car throws a body up and over its hood.
- **Three answers.**
  - The shambler is a crowd problem: drive through them.
  - The sprinter is a reflex problem: it screams, charges a locked line, and you step aside.
  - The Echo is a stealth problem: it copies what it hears and draws more in.
- **Readable warnings.** You hear groans before you see who made them. A sprinter's scream winds it up. A crowd on the move
  kicks up dust.
- **Hordes as events.** A horde migrates down a highway on the world clock, and its dust shows where it is. The radio
  reports it only after someone with a set has seen it.

## 2. Player fantasy
- You put a round through a man's back and he arches, stumbles two steps and goes down on his face.
- You put one through a shambler's knee: the leg comes off, the rest of it drops and keeps dragging toward you.
- One clean shot to the head and it folds where it stood.
- You take the cruiser through a crowd at 60 km/h. Bodies go up over the hood and the windscreen and land behind you.
- A man dies on the stairs, and his body lies on the stairs.
- You shoot one in the back of a pickup, and it is still lying in the bed when the truck drives off.
- The street is quiet, but there is a groan from behind the laundromat you can't see.
- A shambler stops, rears back and screams. You throw yourself sideways and it goes past you like a train, then it's
  bent double and wheezing. That is when you shoot.
- You fire once near the dead mall. Two seconds later your own gunshot comes back from the dark, "BANG", and every shape
  in the lot turns toward the Echo.
- At noon there is a brown smear on the horizon over the interstate. An hour later the trucker on the CB says a few
  hundred are walking west on I-10.

## 3. Detailed rules
### 3.1 The death library (`DEATH_CLIPS`)
- **The 12 death points**:
  - the hips, knees and ankles;
  - the shoulders, elbows and hands;
  - the even index is the left (+x).

  While a clip plays they are body-local: +z forward, feet on y = 0, the lowest point resting at its radius.
- **Sources:**
  - **Baked**: Robert's LSW motion packs, re-solved onto DSOA's rig by `bake-ual.mjs`. Only CC0 is used: the validator
    refuses a proprietary source.
  - **Procedural keys**: per key, `pitch`, `roll`, `yaw`, `lean`, `slide`, the thighs' `flex` and the knees' `bend`, and
    each arm's `[pitch, splay, flex]`. These are solved into the same 12 points at 30 fps when the module loads.
- **The library:**

| Clip | Source | Licence | What it does | Rag / end (s) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| front | UAL `Death01` (fall-supine) | CC0-1.0, Quaternius UAL 1 via LSW `motion.hero-ual` | staggers back and falls on its back, away from the shooter | 1.7 / 2.375 |
| back | own keys | ours | arches at the hit, stumbles, falls forward on its face | 0.62 / 0.95 |
| head | own keys | ours | the knees go first; it folds straight down where it stood | 0.3 / 0.55 |
| leg | own keys | ours | a leg buckles, it goes to its knees, onto its hands, crawls ~0.85 m and stops | 2.6 / 2.9 |
| blast | UAL2 `Hit_Knockback` | CC0-1.0, Quaternius UAL 2 via LSW `motion.hero-ual2` | a knockback frame, then the ragdoll with a 6.5 m/s back, 6.5 m/s up, 4 rad/s spin kick | 0.06 / 0.5 |
| runover | own keys + `HOOD` | ours | swept off its feet; the throw is the ragdoll's (§3.3) | 0 / 0.2 |
| burn | own keys | ours | beats at the flames, staggers, kneels, then curls on its side in the fire's curl | 3.1 / 3.4 |
| blunt | own keys | ours | the head snaps aside, the legs go out sideways | 0.5 / 0.8 |
| blade | own keys | ours | hands to the wound, bent over, to the knees, over on its side | 1.3 / 1.6 |

- **Not used:** `motion.fab-close-combat` is Proprietary-Internal and must never ship. CMU is embed-only and has no
  death take.
- **Which clip plays** (`deaths.pick`, first match wins):
  1. fire → burn
  2. explosion → blast
  3. vehicle → runover
  4. bash: blade (the knife; a blade weapon) or blunt
  5. a head hit → head
  6. a leg hit → leg
  7. otherwise front or back. A round that travelled the way the body faced (forward · shot > `BACK_DOT` 0.2) came from
     behind.
- **The part:**
  - The shooter's part is used when known: `combat.damage`'s `part` or a one-shot `c.hitPart`.
  - Otherwise the infected's own roll (`zombies.onHurt`) is used.
  - Failing both, the part is rolled from `HIT_ODDS` as every hit is.

### 3.2 The ragdoll (`RAGDOLL`, `VEHICLE_TOP`)
- **Links:** the 12 points are tied at the rig's own lengths (`characters.js RIG`). The torso quad is held square by its
  diagonals, and minimum links stop a knee or an elbow folding flat.
- **The step:** a fixed 1/60 s step, so a fall is the same at 20 and at 144 fps:
  - verlet, with air drag;
  - `iters` (4) passes of the links;
  - collision on every pass.
- **The hand-over from the clip:** at the clip's `rag` time the points carry the clip's own speed plus the row's `kick`.
  Until `end` the clip pulls them, `0.22 × (1 − progress)`, so the motion reads as the clip's and lands as physics.
- **What a point lies on** is the highest of:
  - the land, a floor or a stair (`stunts.heightAt`: the storeys' stairs and the underground's flights included);
  - a vehicle's top. Bands along the vehicle give the top at a share of its `tall`: the nose 0.62, the cab 1.0, the
    tail 0.66.
  - a deck's real floor where there is a deck (`decks.floorYCached`): the pickup's bed, the van's cargo, the RV.
- **Contact:**
  - Friction is relative to the surface's own velocity, so a moving hood or bed drags what lies on it.
  - A body thuds; it does not bounce (`bounce` 0.15).
  - The torso and the hands are kept out of walls (`statics.resolveCircle`).
- **Settled:** every point is slower than `settleV` relative to what it lies on for `settleT` s, or `maxT` s (6) have
  passed. The body is then FROZEN. On a vehicle it keeps its place in the vehicle's frame and rides with it. If the
  vehicle is removed, the body falls again.
- **The cap:** at most `maxActive` (16) ragdolls step at once. A 17th freezes the oldest live one where it is.
- **Moved by someone:** a body dragged, harpooned or teleported takes all its points with it. While `c.dragged` the
  ragdoll does not step.
- **Segment probes:** the torso's sides and diagonals, the thighs, shins and upper arms are sampled at a quarter, a half and three quarters of their length. A sample under what is beneath it lifts both ends (and their previous positions, so a lift is not a launch), so a body across stair treads or a kerb rests on the nosings instead of cutting through them. `death action=view` reports `cut`: the worst dip, under 0.03 m on the stairs check.
- **The bodies** are LSW's faceted kit (feat/web-lsw-faceted, merged here): `ragPose` hands its frames to the kit's `drive()` and keeps its own-hair rule.
- **Death rites** keeps working on the same Character: the rot colours come through `paint()`, then the bone pile. The
  frozen pose is what rots.

### 3.3 Run over (`HOOD`)
- On a vehicle kill, the car that did it is found: the nearest one within 9 m moving faster than 2 m/s.
- **At or above `minSpeed` (7 m/s):** every point takes the car's velocity × `carry` (0.62):
  - the legs get `sweep` (3.2 m/s) more along the car;
  - the top of the body gets less;
  - all of it is thrown up at `up` + `upPerMs` × speed (2.4 + 0.16 × v).

  The car outruns the body: it lands on the hood, slides back over the windscreen and roof, and falls off behind.
- **Below `minSpeed`:** it is knocked down, not thrown.
- **The car** gets blood (`blood.splash`).
- **In the air** the body is `rag.air`, and `blood.js` skips it, so there are no smears on the road under a flying body.
- **A fast car** (≥ 14 m/s) takes a leg off a quarter of the time.

### 3.4 Headshots and limbs (`HEADSHOT`, `SEVER`, `SEVERED`)
- **Headshot:** a round (`causes: bullet`) to the head of the infected doing `min` (14) damage or more ends it on that
  hit, whatever its hp, with the head clip. The living die by hp, but a killing head hit still plays the head clip.
- **Sever:** a hit to an arm or a leg past `min` (arms 30, legs 34) takes the limb off `chance` of the time (0.45 / 0.5).
  - A blade cuts limbs off: arms 0.35, legs 0.15.
  - An explosion that kills with ≥ 60 takes 1–3 limbs.
- **On the body:**
  - It is drawn without the limb (`c.lost` bits armL 1, armR 2, legL 4, legR 8; `characters.js hideLost`).
  - An LSW near body with a lost limb steps back to the classic rig, which shows the stump honestly.
  - The stump sprays and leaves a splash.
- **The piece:** it falls as a two-point verlet stick in the sleeve or trouser colour. Pieces are one InstancedMesh (one
  draw call, at most 40), and each stays 900 s.
- **A leg gone** makes one of the living infected a crawler at once (`zombies.crawler`). An arm gone, it still comes.

### 3.5 Blood
- **The pool:** the kill's own pool (`combat.kill`) spreads where it fell. A body that came to rest more than 1.2 m away
  (run over, blown up, crawled) gets a second pool where it lies:
  - 2.0–3.2 m, or 3.8 for a blast or a run-over;
  - laid at the height of what it lies on (a stair, an upstairs floor), on the `blood` depth layer (`fx.decal` y = surface
    − ground + `DECAL_Y`).
- **Smears:** `blood.js` smears any dead body that moves on the ground, as before. It now skips one in the air or on a
  vehicle.
- **No pool on a vehicle** (the car got its splash).

### 3.6 The breed answers (`BREED_ANSWERS`)
| Breed | Answer | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| shambler | CROWD | It latches onto a car only below `latchBelow` 7 m/s (the existing claw rule). A fast car runs it down: 112 dmg at 16 m/s against 75 hp |
| sprinter | REFLEX | **Scream:** on a trigger it rears back (`handsUp`), screams (`WARNINGS.scream.range` 70 m) and stands for `windup` 0.6 s.<br>**Charge:** it runs at 9 m/s along the heading it LOCKED at the end of the scream. It cannot turn until it is `overshoot` 4 m past the locked spot, then at most `steer` 1.1 rad/s. Then the overheat, as before.<br>**Leap:** screaming or charging, it LEAPS onto any moving car within `carLeap` 5.5 m, at any speed (`carCombat.grab`), and claws in through the glass |
| echo | STEALTH | A noise of `minR` 10 m or more within `hear` 60 m comes back from where it stands `delay` 1.4 s later, `gain` 1.0 as loud, at most once every `every` 4 s.<br>The copy is a real noise to the infected (`src: 'echo'`) and to the horde records (half as loud).<br>An Echo never copies a copy. A knife-quiet kill (a 3 m noise) copies nothing |

### 3.7 Warnings (`WARNINGS`)
- **Groans:** every 1.4–3 s, the nearest of the infected you CAN'T see (line of sight; from a car, any line) inside
  50 m groans: a positional sound (pan, distance). With none hidden, the nearest you can see groans. It emits
  `groanHeard {id, dist, seen}`.
- **Scream:** above.
- **Dust:**
  - Twice a second, any crowd of `crowd` 6 or more of the infected moving within `radius` 12 m of each other puffs a pale
    haze cloud over its middle. That is one cloud per crowd, a puff growing 2–8 m over 2.4 s.
  - A migrating horde near the view puffs a plume over its record every 0.35 s, 14–55 m across, rising.
  - All of it is one InstancedMesh of soft quads (one draw call, 120 puffs).

### 3.8 Hordes as events (`MIGRATION`, `systems/migrations.js`)
- **The record:** a migration is an `overrun.js` horde record (`H.route`) that walks a corridor's centreline (I-10, I-95,
  I-75) on the world clock at `speed` 1.5 m/s, for `length` 6 km, then breaks up into an ordinary horde.
- **Near you** its bodies stand up (overrun's materialize, up to 40) and walk the road themselves, wandering up to
  `lane` 18 m across it.
- **A lure:** a big noise draws it off like any horde (overrun's drift). After the linger it walks back to the road and
  on.
- **On the clock:** one rises every 20–40 game hours, 1.8–3.2 km along the road from you, walking toward you, at most 2
  at once. Overrun's `auto` gates it, so it is off in the battery.
- **Law 6:**
  - It is a FACT (`what: horde`) that nobody holds at first.
  - Once a second, a holder with a set inside `witness` m (220 by day, ×0.45 at night) SEES it. That holder is a
    town's lookouts (`town:*`, `radio: true`) or a living person with `c.radio`.
  - You see it yourself inside your sight, or its dust by day inside 2200 m (`saw`).
  - The first witness with a set broadcasts it `report` (6) game minutes later (`knowledge.broadcast`). You get it only
    with a radio inside the broadcast's range, and then the news station reads it out (`radio.say`).
  - Nothing reports a horde nobody has seen.

## 4. Formulas
| Name | Formula | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Back or front | `dot((sin f, cos f), shot) / |shot| > 0.2` → back | Shot from behind, the round travels the way they face: dot 1 → back |
| Clip pull | `P += (clip(t) − P) × 0.22 × (1 − (t − rag) / (end − rag))` | Front at t 2.0: `0.22 × (1 − 0.3/0.675)` = 0.12 per step |
| Throw | `v = car × 0.62 + legs 3.2; vy = 2.4 + 0.16 × speed` | At 18 m/s: body 11.2 m/s, legs 14.4, up 5.3 m/s: ~1.1 s airborne, 7.5 m behind the nose |
| Headshot | `part = head ∧ dmg ≥ 14` → hp 0 | A 17-dmg SMG round to the head ends a 75 hp shambler |
| Sever | `dmg ≥ min ∧ rand < chance` | A 38-dmg revolver round to the leg: legs 34, so 0.5 of the time |
| Sidestep | it passes when `(lock − pos) · head < −4` | A charge from 14 m at 9 m/s reaches the spot in 1.6 s, past it 0.45 s later; 3.5 m aside it never comes within 3.5 m |
| Echo copy | heard if `d < 60 ∧ r ≥ 10`; copy `r × 1.0` at the Echo | A revolver (45 → onNoise 90) at 45 m: shamblers 70 m beyond the Echo hear the copy (70 < 90), never the shot (115 > 90) |
| Migration | `s += dir × 1.5 × tick` | 60 s on the clock = 90 m of I-10 |

## 5. Edge cases
- **A body in a vehicle** (a passenger shot through the glass): no clip. It dies in its seat as before.
- **The player:** no clip (respawn is main.js's).
- **An essential NPC** downed and revived: `c.rag` and the lost limbs are cleared when they stand up.
- **Old saves:** a dead body loaded without `c.rag` lies in the old pose.
- **The ragdoll cap:** in a massacre (more than 16 dying at once) the oldest freeze early. They are frozen in whatever
  frame they reached, never under the ground: every point is kept above the surface on placement.
- **A body on a vehicle** that is scrapped falls to the road again. A frozen body on a car that drives off rides it.
  There is no shake-off yet.
- **Stairs:** the surface query takes the point's own height (+0.25), so a body on a flight lies on the steps, never on
  the floor under the flight.
- **A severed piece** is not loot and not a body. Death rites never sees it, and it goes after 900 s.
- **Echo:** it never copies its own copy or another Echo's, so there is no chain. It only copies once every 4 s.
- **Migration on a corridor's end:** it breaks up into a walking horde (`migrationEnded`).
- **A drift (lure)** suspends the route walk, so overrun runs it.

## 8. Acceptance criteria (selftest: `only="deaths:|infected fun|hordes:"`, 12 checks)
1. **Library:** `validateDeaths` and `validateHordes` are clean. There are nine clips, each with a licence; the baked
   ones are CC0. A missing clip, a proprietary source and two breeds with one answer are each caught.
2. **Every death type:** each of the nine plays its own clip (`death action=play`) and ends SETTLED: frozen, no point
   floating > 0.15 m over what is under it, links within 15%.
3. **Headshot:** a 20-dmg round to the head ends a 75 hp shambler on that hit (head clip, folds < 0.8 m from where it
   stood). The same round to the torso does not.
4. **Leg to crawler:** a 40-dmg leg round (the sever chance forced to 1 for the check) takes the leg off (lost bit, a
   piece on the ground). It is a crawler, alive, prone, and closes > 0.5 m in 4 s.
5. **Over the hood:** you drive an interceptor at 18 m/s into a man. He is killed with the runover clip and thrown more
   than 1.0 m up, OVER the car (higher than the hood inside its footprint, or carried back past its tail). He was
   airborne, no smear was laid while he was, and he settles.
6. **Stairs:** a man shot on a street flight on the island settles ON it: every point rests on its own step (gap
   < 0.15), more than 0.25 m between his lowest and highest point, above the street.
7. **Truck bed:** a man killed standing at a pickup's bed lands on the deck floor (`onVehicle` = the pickup, the lowest
   point at the floor). When the pickup rolls, he rides more than 2 m with it.
8. **Crowd against reflex:** an interceptor at 16 m/s gets more than 45 m through 20 shamblers with none latched. Through
   3 sprinters, at least 2 leap on.
9. **Reflex:** a triggered sprinter is in its wind-up (still, reared back), then charges. You step 3.5 m aside as it
   charges: it runs past its locked spot (`overshoot`), never comes within 1.3 m, and you lose no hp.
10. **Stealth:** a 3 m noise is not copied. A revolver shot at 45 m from an Echo is copied within its delay. Two
    shamblers 70 m beyond the Echo, who never heard the shot, remember the copy (`src: 'echo'`, within 12 m of the Echo)
    and close more than 3 m on it.
11. **Warnings:** one of the infected 32–44 m off behind a wall groans to you, unseen. Eight on the move raise dust; one
    alone does not.
12. **Migration:**
    - It walks I-10 on the clock: 60–140 m in 60 s, never more than 8 m in 2 s, within the 18 m lane of the centreline.
    - With no witness, there is no report and you know nothing.
    - A witness with a set 120 m ahead of it sees it, and the report goes out.
    - You, 2.6 km off in a car (a radio), get a copy by `radio`, and `hordeReported` marks you as one who heard.
