## Patterns

- **Bunker-hop structure**: almost every book opens with a jump into a sealed bunker, then one walk to a real town; so each book yields 1-4 nearby real places rather than a road trip. Exceptions are the long trips in 39 (Florida to Georgia by sea) and 70 (canyon to Kansas City to Denver to Taos by rail).
- **Clusters**: the Virginia home valley (46-48: Norfolk, Front Royal, Casanova, Bull Run, plus Shiloh TN and Green Bank WV), Salt Lake valley (49, 57), Niagara (61), the Carolinas (36, 41, 66), the Pacific Northwest (38, 56, 59, 67) and the desert Southwest (38, 40, 63-65, 70).
- **Recurring book-shape**: a town grown around one surviving pre-war machine or building whose owner rules through it: a mall, a prison, a water pump, an oil field, a refinery, a radio dish, a hospital, a submarine.
- **Military anchors**: Kings Bay, Norfolk, Fort Leavenworth (our pick), White Sands (our pick), Bikini Atoll and the villain's base at Shiloh: six bunkered rows; bunker levels are plausible only unless noted.
- **Off-map**: Puerto Rico (43), Marshall Islands (53-55), Niagara's Canadian side (61), Mexico City and the Amazon (64, 68) - flagged by their state codes (PR, MH, ON, CDMX/MX).

## Gaps and honesty notes

- Short of the 60-row target: 54 rows. Books 36, 52, 60, 62 and 69 name no real place that can be pinned (see `invented_skipped`); padding with guesses would break the no-invented-facts rule.
- Rows whose `source.notes` say **our pick** are a real place chosen to fit a region the book names only loosely: Deadwood and Devils Tower (37), Shark Valley (39), Guadalupe town (40), the Raleigh-Durham hospital campus (41/43), Pine Flat Dam (44), Cambridge OH and the Moundsville prison (50), Fort Leavenworth (51), Bingham Canyon (57), Ranger TX (58), Old Fort Erie (61), White Sands (65), St. Helena Island (66), El Segundo (Encounter).
- Sources: Deathlands fandom wiki (via its API; direct page fetch is blocked with HTTP 402), archived jamesaxler.com wiki plot pages (web.archive.org), worldswithoutend.com blurbs. Some book pages carried only partial plots, so places mentioned deep in a book may be missing.
- Book oddities kept as written: 51 puts Tonganoxie in Missouri (it is in Kansas); 38 puts Carson City in Colorado (not used); 70 runs a train through Taos (no railroad there) and over the canyon (no bridge exists).
- Every `real.refs` URL was fetched this pass (Wikipedia API extracts, plus cinematreasures.org for the Charlotte mall). Layout and building details lean on those articles plus general knowledge of each place as it stood around 2000; storeys are capped at 6 for the miniature (tall towers become stubs).
- Freedom Mall: the opening year and anchor stores are not confirmed by a fetched source; only its location at Freedom Drive and Ashley Road and a 1970s cinema are.
