## Patterns

1. **The late print run rides the map like a road trip of real regions**: 106-125 hit a new state almost every book (Iowa, Montana, Alaska, NH, Maine, Oklahoma x2, California, Detroit, Antarctica, NYC, Mississippi, Nebraska, North Dakota) but name the region, rarely the town, so most rows here are a real town or base we picked to match the book's shape.
2. **The audio era (126-158) stops travelling and goes serial**: long arcs around a few anchors - a Wisconsin home town (Baraboo), the hero's Virginia home valley (Front Royal), Lake Huron, then the East Coast (DC, Baltimore) - with many books naming no real place at all (time travel, book-only towns).
3. **Military sites dominate the bunker logic**: blurbs say only "an old military installation" or "an abandoned base" without naming one; the real anchors are missile fields (Montana, North Dakota), hardened command centers (Offutt, Mount Weather, Cheyenne Mountain) and coastal yards (Portsmouth).
4. **Water is the late series' favourite stage**: Caribbean harbor, Atlantic sub, Mississippi ironclads, Lake Huron pirates and wrecks, Baltimore harbor - good fits for watercraft/diving plans.
5. **Ruined real cities return as faction maps**: Detroit (gang wedges on radial avenues), Washington (city underground), Baltimore (harbor bowl factions), Chicago (elevated loop) - each a ready `grid_downtown`/`port_wharf` set.

## Gaps and honesty notes

- Blurbs only: plot detail came from publisher/retailer blurbs (fandom wiki blocked with HTTP 402). Places inside books beyond the blurb are not captured.
- Many rows are **our pick** of a real place matching a region the blurb names (marked in each row's `source.notes`).
- Every `real.refs` URL was fetched or existence-checked against Wikipedia this pass. 33 rows were confirmed by title/summary only, not read in depth for layout, so their structure lists lean on general knowledge of the place: frederiksted-fort-town, des-moines-capitol-hill, red-rock-dam, ottumwa-river-yards, keokuk-lock-and-dam, fairbanks-chena-downtown, mount-washington-summit, berlin-paper-mill-town, portland-old-port, brunswick-naval-air-station, luray-page-valley-farms, fort-sill-post, detroit-riverfront-towers, detroit-belle-isle, detroit-ambassador-bridge, dearborn-rouge-complex, nyc-times-square, nyc-grand-central, nyc-lower-manhattan-2001, nyc-central-park-south, nyc-brooklyn-bridge, chimney-rock-spire, bismarck-capitol-tower, devils-lake-quartzite-bluffs, wellington-harbor-capital, chicago-loop-elevated, chicago-navy-pier, port-huron-blue-water, dc-capitol-hill, dc-union-station-metro, dc-national-mall, baltimore-inner-harbor, baltimore-fells-point.
