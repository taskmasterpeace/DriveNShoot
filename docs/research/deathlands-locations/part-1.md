## Patterns

- **The Southwest is home base.** New Mexico is the most-visited state (a friend's ranch by a bunker recurs in books 14-16 and 22-24), with Colorado (books 18, 19, 30, 31) and Arizona next; adobe, red-rock canyon and high-desert sites dominate.
- **The Atlantic seaboard is the second pole:** New York City (books 5, 13, 14), the Hudson-Delaware-Shenandoah overland run (book 5), Maine's park islands and mountain bunkers (7, 25, 26) and the cratered capital (27).
- **Recurring place types:** a sealed military bunker under a landmark (lake island, granite mountain, city core, radar base, carved mountain); a tyrant's fortified town built in a reused building (motel, theme park, plantation house, castle, spa hotel); and water set-pieces (blown dam, toll bridge, ferry, riverboat, rapids).
- **Water geography drives most plots:** rivers (Mohawk, Hudson, Delaware, Colorado, Mississippi), lakes (Crater, Superior, Champlain) and island chains (Maine islands, Florida Keys, Channel Islands, Okinawa).
- **Many book places are unnamed or invented but sit in real regions,** so 45 of 81 rows are OUR anchors or route additions (flagged "OUR" in `notes`); two books leave the US (Moscow, Okinawa) and one goes to the Mexican jungle; book 35's setting could not be verified.
